# Potential future features

Deferred by the operator on 29 October 2026. These are not current MVP controls or commitments. Keep them out of the movement picker until their actual requirements are implemented and qualified.

| Movement | Required capability |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking shot | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Follow shot / over-the-shoulder | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Reverse tracking / walk-and-talk | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Side tracking | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Low tracking | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Vehicle tracking | Needs animated vehicle paths. |
| Chase shot | Needs animated subject paths. |
| Body-mounted camera / Snorricam | Needs subject animation and a body attachment. |
| Tilt-shift | Needs a tilt-shift optical effect; ordinary depth of field is different. |
| Infinite zoom | Needs nested scene content for an infinite visual transition. |
| Earth zoom out | Needs city, landscape and planet-scale scene content. |
| Time-lapse | Needs time-varying subjects or lighting. |

Source reviewed: [AI-Verse camera movement library](https://aiverse-filmmakers.github.io/hub/camera-movements/). The current picker implements the 34 camera-only entries plus dolly zoom in/out, parallax zoom in/out and rack focus. First-person currently means camera motion only; animated hands are not included.
